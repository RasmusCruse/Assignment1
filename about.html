About Me:

General Knowledge!

I am 32 years old. I have a background in retail sales, but I felt that I needed a new direction in life. That is why I decided to go back to school and challenge myself by pursuing a career where I can continue to learn and develop. Currently, I work at Tøjeksperten in Storcenter Nord.

I live with my girlfriend in a townhouse just north of Aarhus, together with our two dogs. We have been together for 13 years.

Originally, I come from the greater Copenhagen area, but when I was 12 years old, my parents got divorced, and my dad and I moved to a small town in North Jutland called Vrensted, just outside Løkken.

Personal Life!

Outside of school and work, I enjoy spending time in nature and going to the beach with my two dogs. I really enjoy traveling and I absolutely love Japan, I have visited 3 times within 2 years.

I also enjoy cooking and experimenting in the kitchen. Recently, I have especially gotten into sourdough baking and enjoy trying out different recipes and techniques.

Professional Life

I have several years of professional experience in sales and customer service, where I have developed a solid understanding of responsibility, teamwork, and structured ways of working. I thrive in dynamic environments with a variety of tasks and find it easy to learn and adapt to new systems and processes.
